PWHL Analytics

A connected set of views for reading PWHL hockey at different levels of depth — from the full schedule and game reports to player, goalie, and team dashboards. Use this page as the landing hub, then jump directly into the section you need.

Schedule
Browse every game by season, state, team, date, and status. Open any matchup directly into the full game view.
Report
Shot maps, xG, team filters, on-ice combinations, and report-table breakdowns from the shared data model.
Data
Filter export-ready data views and pull the underlying play-by-play slices when you need raw outputs.
Skaters
Player cards, shot maps, trend charts, and EA integration for individual skater analysis.
Goalies
GSAx, xGA, save-percentage context, and goalie-specific visual breakdowns.
Teams
Team-level dashboards across standings, shooting, play-driving, and roster-level performance splits.
